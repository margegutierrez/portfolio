Case study

Oreve

Faster charging, without friction

Discover the app ↗
CategoryProduct Design
ClientOreve
Year2023
ContextHéméra Digital Consulting

01Context

With the rise of electric vehicles, access to charging has become a major issue for individuals and businesses alike. Yet existing solutions are full of friction: few ultra-fast chargers, complex interfaces, unclear station availability, tedious payments.

Oreve is building a network of ultra-high-power stations (up to 400 kW) in business parks, to guarantee accessibility, speed and reliability.

02The challenge

How do you align acquisition, retention and operational performance in an EV charging product, while reducing friction and maximising adoption of ultra-fast stations?

Goals

  1. 01Offer smooth, intuitive charging to win users and keep them for the long run.
  2. 02Maximise charger use by cutting friction, waiting times and blockers.
  3. 03Meet the concrete needs of every user profile.

03Expected impact

  1. 01+20 to +30% charger usage
  2. 02−25 to −40% waiting time
  3. 03Target NPS above +40
