Case study

Renault

Reinventing how people discover Renault’s iconic models

Discover the app ↗
CategoryProduct Design
ClientRenault
Year2022
ContextAltavia Disko agency

01Context

The by reno app modernises how people discover Renault’s new iconic models, the Renault 4 and Renault 5, and paves the way for upcoming models. It is part of the brand’s digital transformation and community engagement strategy.

A ChatGPT-based conversational assistant guides each user in real time: vehicles, tech innovations, personalisation options, differences from the historic models.

02The challenge

How do you deliver an immersive, engaging car experience while keeping key information quick and easy to reach?

Goals

  1. 01Build engagement around the iconic models and strengthen the brand image.
  2. 02Immerse users through a rich, interactive digital experience.
  3. 03Simplify discovery to make understanding and deciding easier.

03Expected impact

  1. 01+30 to +50% time spent in the experience
  2. 0240 to 60% of users chat with the assistant
  3. 03+15 to +25% high-intent actions (information, test drive, contact)

  3. 03Personas and user journey map
